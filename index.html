Child Safety Standards

Application: the BOSS — Orange Jordan
Developer: Orange Jordan Apps

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Orange Jordan Apps is the developer of the BOSS — Orange Jordan (the “App”), distributed on Google Play. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and for child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These standards apply to every user of the App and to us as its developer.

Prohibited content and conduct

The following are strictly forbidden in the App and are acted on without exception:

  • Creating, uploading, storing, sharing, requesting, or linking to child sexual abuse material, in any format, including drawn, animated, computer-generated, or AI-generated depictions.
  • Sexualizing a minor in any content — text, images, audio, video, usernames, display names, profile details, or any other user-generated content.
  • Grooming, sextortion, or any attempt to solicit, coerce, or lure a minor for sexual purposes.
  • Child trafficking, or advertising, promoting, or facilitating it.
  • Promoting, glorifying, or normalizing sexual interest in minors, or providing instructions for any of the conduct above.
  • Sharing or seeking contact details, off-platform links, or resources intended to facilitate any of the conduct above.

How users can report

Anyone can report suspected CSAE content or behaviour to us at any time by emailing the child safety contact listed below. Reports of this kind are treated as urgent and are reviewed ahead of all other reports. We accept reports from users, members of the public, regulators, and law enforcement.

How we respond

  • We review reports of CSAE promptly and prioritize them above all other moderation queues.
  • Confirmed violating content is removed immediately and the account is permanently terminated.
  • We preserve the relevant content and account data as required by applicable law, so that authorities can investigate.
  • We report apparent CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) through its CyberTipline, and to the competent authorities in the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan, as required by applicable law.
  • Accounts terminated for CSAE violations are not reinstated.

Compliance with law

Orange Jordan Apps complies with child safety and child protection laws applicable in the jurisdictions where the App is distributed, including any legal obligation to report child sexual abuse material to the relevant authorities.

Age and audience

The App is intended for users aged 18 and above, consistent with the target audience and content rating declared on our Google Play store listing. Access to the App is limited to verified Orange Jordan personnel through an authenticated sign-in; the App does not offer open public registration.

Review of these standards

We review these standards at least annually, and whenever the App adds features that change how users can create content or communicate with each other.
